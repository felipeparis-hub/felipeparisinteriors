Why choose me as your interior designer in Marbella: showroom, video calls and more

Why work with me: a showroom in Marbella, video calls if you live abroad, Roche Bobois stores in many countries, renders and the design included.

By Felipe Paris, interior designer in Marbella

Go to my website

When you search for an interior designer in Marbella you find plenty of options: large studios, freelance decorators, furniture shops offering design services. It is natural to wonder who to work with. Here is, honestly, what I offer and how my way of working is different.

1. A showroom in Marbella where you can see and touch every piece

Buying a sofa from a photo is risky. The comfort of a seat, the drape of a fabric or the real tone of a wood can only be understood in person. That is why I welcome you at the Roche Bobois showroom in Marbella: you can sit on the sofas, compare fabrics and finishes, see the pieces at full scale and get an exact idea of how they will look in your home.

The visit is not a quick sale. You come with the floor plan or a few photos, we go through every room together and you leave with a clear direction: which pieces, in which sizes and with which materials.

2. If you live abroad, we work by video call

Many of my clients live in other countries and cannot fly in every time a decision is needed. For them I work by video call: I show you the pieces and fabrics live from the showroom, we review the proposal together on screen and answer your questions without you having to catch a plane. I explain this in detail in my guide to furnishing your Marbella home from abroad.

3. Roche Bobois stores in many countries

Roche Bobois has stores in many countries across Europe, the Americas and Asia. So if you live in another city, you can visit the store nearest to your home to sit on the sofa I am proposing or see a fabric in person. Then we carry on with the project together, with me as your point of contact for your Marbella home.

4. See your finished home before you decide

I do not ask you to imagine anything. I prepare renders on real photos of your home, with your light and your views, and the proposed pieces in place. You can see an example in the villa project in Elviria, which started from a house still under works.

5. Design included, furniture at the official price

The first consultation is free, and the full design project, with layout and renders, is included when you furnish with Roche Bobois. You pay the official furniture price, with no extra design fees.

6. Training and local knowledge

I spent five years with Roche Bobois in France, in the showroom and at furniture fairs, and more than three years in Costa del Sol real estate. I know the collections inside out and I know how villas are lived in, area by area, from Sierra Blanca to La Zagaleta. If you are still choosing where to buy, my guide to furnishing a villa in Marbella area by area may help.

7. In your language, from start to finish

I speak English, Spanish, French and Italian. You have a single point of contact who knows your home, the plan and the budget, and who coordinates measurements, order, delivery and placement. If the villa is a rental, we plan for that from day one, as I explain in my holiday rental guide.

How we start

Send me a few photos or the floor plan of your home on WhatsApp and tell me whether you prefer to visit the Marbella showroom or have a video call. I will prepare a first proposal in your language.
