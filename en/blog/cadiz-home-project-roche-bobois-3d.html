Case study in Cádiz: a whole home in Roche Bobois, decided in 3D

A client in Cádiz furnished her living room, dining room and bedroom with Roche Bobois after seeing them in 3D. The pieces and a budget of about €50,000.

By Felipe Paris, interior designer specialising in Roche Bobois in Marbella

Go to my website

Some clients arrive with a list of furniture; others arrive with a whole home to solve. This project is one of the second kind: a home in Cádiz with a living room, dining room and master bedroom to furnish, and an owner who knew exactly which style she liked, but not how everything would look together.

In the end she ordered almost the entire home from Roche Bobois, with a total budget of around €50,000. What convinced her was not a catalogue: it was seeing her own home furnished in 3D, room by room, before signing anything. Here is how we worked and which pieces she chose.

The starting point

First came the floor plan and the real measurements of each room. With those I drew every piece to scale, using its official dimensions, and checked the clearances: how much space is left between the bed and the wardrobes, between the sofa and the coffee table, between the dining table and the wall. It is the least glamorous part of the job, but the most important one. A beautiful sofa that blocks the way is a bad sofa.

Then came the images. Not one closed proposal, but several options to compare in the same room.

The living room: twelve combinations to choose one

For the living room I prepared twelve images: three different sofas, two sets of coffee tables and two rugs, combined with each other in the same room. That way my client could see at a glance what changed from one option to the next, without having to imagine it.

She chose this composition:

  • Préface sofa, L-shaped, 337 × 273 cm: a 3-seater with right arm (L.231) and a 2-seater (L.190), with an 83 × 83 cm ottoman to close the corner and four back cushions with bolster. All in Discours Ecru fabric, warm and easy to live with.
  • Quintet coffee tables by Maurizio Manzoni: a low one in travertine (119 × 84 cm) and a taller one in Canaletto walnut (80 × 69 cm), which overlap and bring depth to the arrangement.
  • Fusion Midnight rug, 250 × 350 cm, with just enough contrast so the light sofa does not disappear against the wooden floor.

The dining room

In the dining room my client wanted to keep her chairs, so the proposal focused on the table and the light. We chose the oval Speed Up table, 200 × 100 cm, by Sacha Lakic, with a Canaletto walnut top and a matt black lacquered base. The oval shape softens the room and makes it easier to move around.

Above it, the Parabola pendant by Cristián Mohaded in champagne, 120 cm long: a sculptural piece that lights the table and, when switched off, is still a beautiful object. Before deciding, we compared two other lamps in the same render.

The master bedroom: fourteen versions to the final one

The bedroom was the room that evolved the most: fourteen versions in all. We tried two different beds, ottoman colours, lamps, the wood cladding on the TV wall and the position of the sideboard. Every change could be seen in the image straight away, which made it possible to decide calmly.

The final version:

  • En-Tête bed, King size with storage, upholstered in Orsetto Ivoire fabric, with a King-size Tendresse mattress.
  • Large Bubble ottoman by Sacha Lakic, 150 × 80 cm, in Orsetto Flex Ivoire, at the foot of the bed.
  • Two Elanta bedside tables by Patrick Norguet, Ø50 cm, in brushed Alezan oak with Onyx handles.
  • Raised Elanta sideboard with 3 doors, L.180 cm, matching the bedside tables.
  • Round Abysse rug by Fritsch & Durisotti, Ø300 cm, in brown wool: its gradient recalls ripples on water and wraps around the bed.

Why 3D changes the decision

Furnishing a whole home is a significant investment, and doubts are normal. Will the sofa fit? Is the rug too dark? Will the ottoman work with the bed? When those questions are answered on screen, the order is placed with peace of mind and there are no surprises on delivery day.

In this project, my client saw every room from several angles, compared options and asked for changes until everything felt right. Then the order went out in one go: living room, dining room and bedroom.

A note of honesty: the images in this article are indicative renders generated with AI from the project. They help to visualise the atmosphere; the final shapes, sizes and finishes are those of each piece's official product sheet. The plans, however, are drawn to scale with the real measurements.
